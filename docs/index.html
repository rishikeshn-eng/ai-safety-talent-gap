Who is AI safety trying to hire?

Counting the open roles at AI safety organisations, every day.

In plain words

AI safety groups often say they can't find enough people. I wanted to see that in numbers. This page reads their job boards every day and counts what is open, what kind of work it is, and how long it has been waiting.

Finding 1

What kind of people are they looking for?

Operations here means the roles that keep an organisation running: people and hiring, finance, legal, IT, events, programme management and chief-of-staff work.

Finding 2

How long have these roles been open?

Read this with care. It only counts roles that are still open today. A role that was filled in two weeks is already gone from the list, so these numbers lean long. Some adverts also stay up all year on purpose.

Finding 3

What do operations adverts ask for?

This is keyword matching on the part of each advert that describes the role. Text that an organisation repeats in every advert, such as benefits, is removed first. It shows what gets mentioned, which is a rough guide to what gets used.

Finding 4

Who is hiring?

For Anthropic and OpenAI the tracker keeps only roles whose title or team mentions safety, safeguards, alignment, interpretability, policy or similar. Their other openings are left out, and their numbers stay out of the headline counts above.

Method

How this is counted, and where it falls short

How it works

  • Once a day a script reads the public job feed of each organisation. These feeds are the same ones that power their careers pages.
  • Each role is sorted into a kind of work by the words in its title.
  • "Days open" runs from the date the advert was first published to today.
  • A daily count is saved, so the trend chart fills in over time and closing dates can be worked out later.

Limits

  • It covers the organisations whose job systems can be read openly. Many others in the field, including several large ones, post jobs in ways this tracker can't read yet.
  • An open advert shows demand. It can't show how many good people applied, so it can't prove a shortage by itself.
  • Sorting by title makes mistakes. A "Program Lead" can be research in one place and operations in another.
  • Days open leans long, for the reasons given under Finding 2.
  • Salaries appear only where the organisation publishes them in its feed.
